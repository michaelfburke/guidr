How it works

Three steps to a finished guide

No manual writing. No copying screenshots one by one. Just record, enrich, and export.

1

Record

Click record and walk through your product normally. Guidr automatically captures each click as a step — screenshot, DOM context, and all.

2

Enrich

Hit "Enrich all" and your chosen AI model generates a title, description, and voiceover script for every step — in seconds.

3

Edit & Export

Refine any step, add annotations or GIF clips, then export as Markdown, HTML, Intercom-ready HTML, or JSON.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Built for customer success teams who need to ship guides fast, not fiddle with tooling.

Click-to-record capture

Every click becomes a chapter marker. Screenshots are compressed automatically to keep AI costs low.

AI-generated content

Step titles, descriptions, and voiceover scripts written for you. Inject your brand voice with tone guides and few-shot examples.

Annotations

Draw circles, arrows, and highlights directly on screenshots. Mask sensitive information before publishing.

Animated GIFs

Trim a clip from your recording and generate a looping GIF for any step. Frame rate and quality are configurable.

Flexible export

Markdown, self-contained HTML, Intercom-ready HTML, or raw JSON. Drop guides anywhere without reformatting.

Full script generation

Stitch every step's voiceover into a single cohesive narration script — complete with intro, transitions, and outro.

Export formats

Paste it anywhere

Self-contained exports work out of the box in every tool your team uses.

Markdown

Base64-embedded screenshots. Works in GitHub, Notion, and any Markdown renderer.

HTML

Single self-contained file. Email-safe and Notion-paste friendly.

Intercom HTML

Paste directly into Intercom help center articles. No reformatting needed.

JSON

Raw structured data for backups or feeding into your own publishing workflow.

Privacy

Your data stays yours

Guidr was designed so you never have to wonder where your recordings go.

Zero servers

The developer sees none of your data. There is no Guidr backend.

Local-only storage

Recordings and settings live in your browser (IndexedDB + chrome.storage).

Bring your own key

Your API key goes directly from your browser to your chosen AI provider.

No telemetry

No analytics, crash reports, or usage tracking of any kind.

Free to install
